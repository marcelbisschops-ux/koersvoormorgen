The platform

One transaction. One file. From preparation to closing.

A virtual dataroom covers the document exchange during the book examination. Useful, but it is one link in the chain. Koers voor Morgen runs the whole transaction: the same environment and the same file, from the first preparation until after the signature.

The core

All parties, one environment

Buyer, seller, adviser and read-only observers work in the same file, each with their own view.

Dataroom per DD category

Financial, commercial, people & partners, legal & tax, IT. Every document lands in its phase, in its place.

Q&A and actions on the documents

Every question and open action hangs on the document it is about; discussions and decisions in the same context.

Roles & phase access

Everyone sees only what fits their role and the current phase. The adviser's internal notes stay internal.

Progress per phase

Coverage, signals and open points in one view, visible to everyone working on it.

The intelligence layer

Document coverage

Per phase, a list of what is expected and what has come in. Gaps are visible before they slow the transaction down.

Traceable to the source

Every value carried over is clickable down to the page in the source document. No retyped numbers without an origin.

AI signals

Missing documents, inconsistencies between documents, open actions. Flagged in the context of the file, never as an assumption.

Optional modules

Contracts from the file

NDA, LOI, indicative bid and exclusivity, drafted from the data already in the file.

Reporting & export

The full file as an ordered package, or an interim report per phase.

Read-only observers

A financier or colleague reads along without changing anything; access revocable at any moment.

Anonymous buyer matching (beta)

Within a running transaction, reach extra buyers on sector and region.

Your own house style

Platform name, accent colour, logo, address and registration number. Your clients work in your environment.

An adviser runs the platform independently for their own clients, under their own name, with a transaction limit and modules of their choosing. You are a client of the platform; nobody from Koers voor Morgen needs to be involved to use it.

AI that guards the file

The AI signals. You decide.

The platform reads along with the file and gives three kinds of signal, and nothing more. It does not fill in fields and does not make assumptions.

Missing document

"For phase 2 the lease for the office is missing." A gap in the coverage, named before it becomes a problem.

Inconsistency between documents

"Revenue in the annual accounts differs from the client overview." The platform puts the documents side by side; the adviser judges.

Action required

"This question has been open for 9 days." Open points and actions stay visible until they are done.
